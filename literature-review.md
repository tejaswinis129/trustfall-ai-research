# Literature Review: TRUSTFALL AI

## Purpose

This review will examine existing work related to securing AI agents that process external information, retain memory, and call tools. The goal is to identify established defenses and define a specific, testable contribution for TRUSTFALL AI.

## Research questions

1. How do direct and indirect prompt-injection attacks affect AI applications and agents?
2. What security risks arise when agents use retrieved content or persistent memory across sessions?
3. How can provenance or data-lineage tracking help connect an untrusted source to a later action?
4. Which actions should require independent verification or human approval?
5. How can defenses be evaluated using benign and adversarial test cases?

## Proposed research gap to investigate

TRUSTFALL AI will investigate whether combining source provenance, cross-session influence tracking, and policy enforcement for sensitive actions improves traceability and defensive outcomes compared with prompt-only checks in a controlled prototype.

This is a hypothesis to test, not a claim that the approach is entirely new or guaranteed to outperform existing systems.
